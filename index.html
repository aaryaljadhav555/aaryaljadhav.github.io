What I've Built

⭐ Featured Project — Yuva AI for All

MIT VPU Buddy

An intelligent AI chatbot built for MIT Vishwaprayag University students. VPU Buddy handles all exam-related queries — schedules, syllabus, hall tickets, results, academic policies — giving students instant, accurate answers 24/7 without having to navigate the college portal.

Built using n8n as the low-code automation backbone, the bot connects to university data sources, parses queries intelligently, and delivers context-aware responses via a conversational interface. Developed as part of the Nasscom Yuva AI for All initiative.
